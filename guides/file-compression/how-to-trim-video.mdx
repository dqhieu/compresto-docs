## Overview

You can trim a video in the main window before compressing it, so the output only keeps the part you need. Each video in a batch can have its own trim.

## Trim a Video

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Add one or more videos to the main window
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Hover over the video and press **Trim video**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Drag the yellow handles on the timeline to set the start and end, then press **Trim**. Press **Cancel** to keep the whole video
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose your compression options and press **Compress**
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a trim, press **Trim video** again. The trim is kept until you load new files or remove the video.

## Good to Know

- A trimmed video is always saved, even if the output isn't smaller than the original. See [Original kept](/guides/file-compression/output-options#original-kept)
- Trims apply to every video output format except GIF, including [MP3 export](/guides/file-compression/how-to-extract-mp3-from-video), and the audio stays in sync
- Trims don't apply when the output format is **GIF**. To make a GIF from part of a video, trim the clip first (for example in QuickTime Player), then [convert it](/guides/file-compression/how-to-convert-video-to-gif)
- Trimming is only available in the main window. Drop Zone, folder monitoring, and deep links compress the whole video
