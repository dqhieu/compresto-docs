## Activate

Paste the license key from your purchase email into **Settings** > **License** and press **Activate**. You can also do this during [setup](/guides/getting-started/setting-up).

Don't have a key? Get one at [compresto.app/pricing](https://compresto.app/pricing).

<Tip>
**Setapp users**: Your license is managed by Setapp, so there's no key to enter.
</Tip>

## What You'll See

| Row | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **License status** | Whether your license is valid. Press the refresh button to check again |
| **License expiry** or **Updates available until** | For one-time licenses: **Never**, or the date your free updates end |
| **Renews on** / **Cancels on** | For subscriptions: the next renewal date, or when the subscription ends |
| **License usage** | How many of your license's devices are activated |
| **License key** and **Email** | Your key and purchase email, with copy buttons |
| **Provider** | Where you bought the license |

If your update period has ended, the License tab says **You're no longer able to receive new updates**. Press **Renew license** to get updates again.

## Manage License

Press **Manage license** to open [compresto.app/manage-license](https://compresto.app/manage-license) with your key already filled in. There you can see your devices, remove ones you no longer use, and manage your purchase.

## Move to Another Mac

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the old Mac, open **Settings** > **License** and press **Unlink device**. This frees up one device on your license
  </Step>
  <Step>
    On the new Mac, install Compresto and activate it with the same key
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Lost access to the old Mac? Remove it at [compresto.app/manage-license](https://compresto.app/manage-license) instead.
</Note>

## Using the Command-Line Tool

The Mac app and the [compresto command-line tool](/cli/introduction) share one activation on the same Mac, so together they count as one device:

- If you signed in to the command-line tool first, the app reuses that activation when you activate it with the same key, or when it launches without a license
- **Unlink device** in the app also signs the command-line tool out on that Mac

<Note>
The app reusing the command-line tool's activation, and signing it out on unlink, is new after version 1.32. In 1.32, activate the app first, then run `compresto auth login`.
</Note>

See [CLI license](/cli/license) for the command-line side.

## Troubleshooting

- **Key not accepted**: Copy the key straight from your purchase email and make sure it's a Compresto key (format `XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX`)
- **Activation limit reached**: Unlink a device you no longer use, or remove it at [compresto.app/manage-license](https://compresto.app/manage-license)
- **Offline**: Activating and checking your license need an internet connection. If a check fails because you're offline, your license stays active
