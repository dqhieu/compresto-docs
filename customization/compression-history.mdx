## Overview

Compresto keeps a history of your compressed files, with a running total of how much space you've saved.

## Open History

- Press **⌘Y**
- Or choose **Compression history** from the Compresto menu
- Or choose **History** from the menu bar icon

## What You'll See

- **Total compressed files** and **Total reduced size** across all your compressions
- For each file: the original and compressed size, and how much was saved (in size and percent)

Click an item to show it in Finder.

## Large Histories

History loads the newest items first and loads more as you scroll. Use the **Show** picker next to **Latest compression** to choose how many items load at a time: 50, 100 (default), 200, or 500.

## Turn Off History

Turn off **Keep compression history** in the History window to stop recording.

<Warning>
Turning off history also clears your existing history.
</Warning>

<Note>
History is stored locally on your Mac and never uploaded.
</Note>
